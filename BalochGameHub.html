Call of Duty®: Modern Warfare® II

First-person Shooter
Metacritic Score: 91/100

Modern Warfare II is the sequel to 2019's blockbuster Modern Warfare. The game features a gripping single-player campaign, immersive multiplayer modes, and the new Special Ops missions.

  • Release Date: October 28, 2022
  • Developer: Infinity Ward
  • Publisher: Activision
  • Platforms: PC, PlayStation, Xbox
Game Features:
  • Continues the gripping narrative from Modern Warfare (2019)
  • All-new gameplay features and advancements
  • New tactical gameplay mechanics
  • Cutting-edge graphics and audio

About the Game

Call of Duty: Modern Warfare II is a first-person shooter game developed by Infinity Ward and published by Activision. It is the nineteenth game in the Call of Duty series and the sequel to 2019's Modern Warfare. The game features a single-player campaign that continues the story of Task Force 141 as they face off against a new threat. The multiplayer mode builds upon the systems from the 2019 reboot, introducing new maps, modes, and gameplay mechanics.

Modern Warfare II also introduces a new cooperative Special Ops mode that continues the narrative from the campaign with missions designed for two players.

The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom

Adventure
Metacritic Score: 96/100

The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom is the sequel to the critically acclaimed Breath of the Wild. This installment takes the open-world adventure to new heights with expanded exploration mechanics and deeper storytelling.

  • Release Date: May 12, 2023
  • Developer: Nintendo EPD
  • Publisher: Nintendo
  • Platforms: Nintendo Switch
Game Features:
  • Expanded open world with sky islands and underground areas
  • New Ultrahand and Fuse abilities for creative problem-solving
  • Deeper story exploring Hyrule's ancient past
  • Enhanced physics and chemistry systems

About the Game

In Tears of the Kingdom, Link explores both the skies above Hyrule and a massive subterranean world below it. The game builds upon the physics-based gameplay of Breath of the Wild while introducing new mechanics that allow for unprecedented creativity in solving puzzles and overcoming obstacles.

The story follows Link as he investigates a mysterious gloom spreading across Hyrule, uncovering secrets about the ancient Zonai civilization and the origins of the Master Sword.

Elden Ring

Action RPG
Metacritic Score: 94/100

Elden Ring is a fantasy action-RPG adventure set in a world co-created by Hidetaka Miyazaki (Dark Souls series) and George R.R. Martin (A Song of Ice and Fire). Explore the Lands Between and become the Elden Lord.

  • Release Date: February 25, 2022
  • Developer: FromSoftware
  • Publisher: Bandai Namco
  • Platforms: PC, PlayStation, Xbox
Game Features:
  • Vast open world with dynamic weather and day-night cycle
  • Deep character customization with numerous weapons and spells
  • Challenging combat with strategic options
  • Rich lore and environmental storytelling

About the Game

Elden Ring combines FromSoftware's signature gameplay with an expansive open world. Players create their own character and set out to explore the Lands Between to repair the Elden Ring and become the new Elden Lord.

The game features challenging combat, intricate level design, and a mysterious narrative that unfolds through exploration and discovery rather than traditional storytelling.

Assassin's Creed Valhalla

Action RPG
Metacritic Score: 84/100

Assassin's Creed Valhalla is an action role-playing video game set in 873 AD during the Viking invasion of Britain. Players control Eivor, a Viking raider who becomes embroiled in the conflict between the Brotherhood of Assassins and the Templar Order.

  • Release Date: November 10, 2020
  • Developer: Ubisoft Montreal
  • Publisher: Ubisoft
  • Platforms: PC, PlayStation, Xbox, Stadia
Game Features:
  • Massive open world set in Dark Ages England and Norway
  • Dual-wielding combat system with a variety of weapons
  • Settlement building and management
  • Choices that affect the story and world

About the Game

In Assassin's Creed Valhalla, players take on the role of Eivor, a Viking raider leading their clan from Norway to settle in England. The game blends history with mythology as Eivor becomes involved in the ancient conflict between the Assassins and Templars.

Valhalla introduces new mechanics to the series including raids, settlement building, and a revamped combat system that emphasizes brutal Viking warfare while maintaining the stealth elements the series is known for.
